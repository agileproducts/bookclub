# The 5 Dysfunctions of a Team

* The only true measure of a team is to achieve results.
* Members of a team can't have different success metrics.


The five dysfuncions:

1. **Absence of Trust**. Great teams can be honest with each other, challenge each other and admit vulnerability. Lack of debate is a symptom.

2. **Fear of Conflict**. Sense of artificial harmony. Tension but no constructive conflict.

3. **Lack of Commitment*. Failure to buy into decisions. People don’t get on board if they don’t think they have been heard. It’s not about consensus, you can’t please everyone. Teams paralysed by need for agreement. Disagree and commit. 

4. **Avoidance of accountability**. Not wanting to criticise people. Avoid discomfort. People won’t hold each other accountable if they never bought in in the first place.

5. **Inattention to collective results**. Individuals only thinking about themselves. Make the collective ego greater than the individual ones. Sports are good for teamwork because they have a clear metric, the score.
